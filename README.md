## ✦  Reflections

I saw her again today,  
through broken pieces left of what was once the window bay.  
She stared,  
gray against the anomaly of the broken scenery,  
and beckoned me with eyes that reflected years of deep melancholy.  

I smiled,  
and through her eyes, I saw the child that once was,  
the whimsical child with clipped-off claws.  
But there was something there now that never was,  
a look in her eyes that reflected hope in my cause.  

I tried to reach her but stood spellbound,  
I tried to call her, but made no sound.  
In my hesitation, she evaporated, morphing into something recent.  
She was a reflection of my past and my future, but was also me in the present.

## ✦ If Shadows Could Talk

If shadows could talk, they would whisper of the horror veiled in a broken child’s whimpers,  
The pain beneath her eyes as she hid her trauma from the judging eyes of the onlookers,  
The ache she hid in silent muffled sobs from being abused,  
And the fear of not being believed and instead becoming the accused.  

They would recall the time the world left her crushed and beat,  
Torn and scarred, with no soul around to cool the heat,  
The silence of the adults who should have protected her,  
But instead left her in the lonely mercy of despair to wither and suffer.  

The times she lay in the dark with tears running down her cheeks from that one encounter,  
Her head reeling with questions she tried and failed not to ponder,  
“Had it really happened or was it all in my head?”  
“It couldn’t be if I hadn’t really bled.”  

They would scream of the ache that jammed its fangs into the shards of her soul,  
The shame she bore for a situation she couldn’t control,  
And comfort her that life hadn’t ended even though she had been defiled,  
If shadows could talk, they would speak up for that broken child.
